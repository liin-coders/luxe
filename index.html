Bridal Protocol

01. How far in advance should I reserve bridal trousseau dates?

Due to our individualized attention and private suite commitments, we strongly recommend reserving your bridal couture dates 2 to 4 months prior. This ensures your dedicated lead stylist is exclusively allocated to you, alongside a preliminary hair texture diagnostic and custom skin brightening regime.
